Language and layout

  • English is the default. The Korean/English switch appears only when the visitor country is detected as South Korea.
  • Translate interface labels and generated summaries of counts. Preserve source titles, summaries and personal writing.
  • Allow English labels to wrap. Do not reduce the font to squeeze them into Korean-sized controls.
  • At 700px and below, stack page panels. At 760px and below, the AIM record explorer uses category/result tabs. The policy explorer keeps filter chips above its scrollable results.
  • Wheel scrolling moves the page. Left drag rotates the globe; right vertical drag zooms. Horizontal right drag does not rotate it.
  • Keep keyboard focus visible and respect reduced-motion preferences.

Policy overview and exploration

The risk and policy panels share country headings, count typography, surfaces and actions. Put the policy exploration action before the category breakdown. Keep the language and map controls centred below the shared action banner.

Policy status colours indicate Active, In development, Completed and Not started. Always show labels and counts alongside colour. These are inventory statuses, not measures of policy effectiveness or legal enforcement.

Filter by status, category and sector, then open a policy record and carry its title and source into a question. On mobile, chip rows scroll horizontally and records scroll within the dialog. An unavailable or incomplete response must never appear as zero policies.

Incident to inquiry

Use four connected steps: incident, policy, evidence gaps, and investigation. Keep the selected incident visible while reviewing policies. Country membership and shared AI principles are discovery clues, not confirmed policy coverage.

Show coverage, implementation and effectiveness as separate questions requiring evidence. Never turn an absent match into a claim that no policy exists. Carry the incident source, policy candidate, connection clues and selected questions into the local inquiry record. Keep Close and the next action visible while the modal body scrolls.

Keep the system and app in sync

design-system.css owns the shared tokens and component rules. The app and this page load that file directly. Existing CSS files keep screen-specific layout rules.

  1. Change a shared token or component rule first.
  2. Review the live example here and the real application screen.
  3. Check Korean and English, desktop and mobile, keyboard focus and empty/error states.
  4. When adding interface copy, update i18n-en.json in the same change.

Map geometry and data-category palettes keep their own semantics. This page is a reference and does not change saved questions, country selection or API settings.
