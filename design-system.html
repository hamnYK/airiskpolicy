Policy overview and exploration

The risk and policy panels share country headings, count typography, surfaces and actions. Put the policy exploration action before the category breakdown. Keep the language and map controls centred below the shared action banner.

Policy status colours indicate Active, In development, Completed and Not started. Always show labels and counts alongside colour. These are inventory statuses, not measures of policy effectiveness or legal enforcement.

Filter by status, category and sector, then open a policy record and carry its title and source into a question. On mobile, chip rows scroll horizontally and records scroll within the dialog. An unavailable or incomplete response must never appear as zero policies.

Incident to inquiry

Use four connected steps: incident, policy, evidence gaps, and investigation. Keep the selected incident visible while reviewing policies. Country membership is a discovery clue. Gap Analyzer candidates require evidence from the published ontology; matching labels alone never bypass it. Neither establishes confirmed policy coverage.

Show coverage, implementation and effectiveness as separate questions requiring evidence. Never turn an absent match into a claim that no policy exists. Carry the incident source, policy candidate, connection clues and selected questions into the local inquiry record. Keep Close and the next action visible while the modal body scrolls.

TOP 10 means up to ten selected issues per category, not a quota. Publish at least one reviewed issue in total; RISK 6 / POLICY 0 is valid. Show actual counts in both tabs. An empty category means no issues were selected, not that no risks or policies exist.
